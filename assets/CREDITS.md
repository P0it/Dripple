# Audio credits

## Sound effects — `assets/sounds/`

From **Kenney Interface Sounds** (https://kenney.nl/assets/interface-sounds),
released under **CC0 1.0** (public domain). No attribution is required; it is
given here as a courtesy. Files were selected per game event and transcoded
from OGG to mono 96 kbps MP3.

## Music — `assets/music/`

- `victory.mp3`, `defeat.mp3` — from **Kenney Music Jingles**
  (https://kenney.nl/assets/music-jingles), **CC0 1.0**.
- `menuTheme.mp3`, `gamePlay.mp3`, `myTurn.mp3`, `tenseMoment.mp3` — generated
  for this project by additive synthesis (`docs/audio/synth.py`). Seamless
  8-bar loops. Original work, no third-party rights attached.

Replacing any of these with commissioned or licensed music is a drop-in
swap: keep the filename, keep the format.
